SOIL TESTING

Soil analysis, priced honestly.

Two tiers depending on how precise you need the numbers. Priced per composite sample — a composite is one representative sample built from many cores across a zone (explained below). Get an instant estimate with the calculator.

What is a composite sample?

A single reading from one spot tells you very little — soil varies across a field. So a composite sample is built from roughly 15 cores taken across a zone in a W-pattern, blended and mixed into one representative sample that reflects the average condition of that area.

A uniform hectare is usually one composite. Where land clearly varies — different soil, slope, or history — it's split into zones, each sampled as its own composite so real differences show up rather than being averaged away.
